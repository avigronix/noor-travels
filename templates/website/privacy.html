How we use and keep your data

Summary – the full text will be confirmed with {{ biz.name }} and its legal adviser.

What we collect

Your name, father's name, date of birth, address, mobile number, passport details, visa details and the payments you make – only what is needed to process your visa or ticket.

How we use it

To book your appointments, submit applications on the official portals, arrange your ticket, send you WhatsApp updates (only if you agree) and give you receipts. We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

How we keep it safe

Your records are kept in private storage that only our logged-in staff can open. Every change is logged. Backups are taken daily and kept for 30 days. We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop WhatsApp updates, or to see or correct your details, by calling {{ biz.phones | join(' or ') }} or visiting the office.
