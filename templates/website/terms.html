Service terms

Summary – the signed policy you receive at registration is the one that applies.

Our service

We help you with the services you choose at registration. Visa decisions, medical results, test results and appointment dates are made by the embassies, centres and authorities, not by us.

Charges and receipts

Your package is written down at registration as our service charge plus costs paid to centres, agencies and airlines. You get a receipt for every payment. Our service charge for work visas stays within the limit set by the Emigration Rules. A failed medical or skill test needs a new attempt, which has its own centre fee.

Refund on cancellation

If you cancel, the refund is: amount received − costs already paid to centres, agencies or airlines − the part of the service charge earned up to the stage reached. The owner approves each refund and you get a written calculation.

Your passport

Every hand-over and courier of your passport is recorded. Please collect it when we tell you it is back in the office.
